ℹ️ Scope of these verdicts
Every verdict in this report covers the 34 requirements this tool can test automatically. Req 1 and Req 2 (OGC base conformance) require the OGC CITE TeamEngine test suites, and Req 6 (conditional extensions) depends on the applicable DGIWG product profile — all three are reported as SKIPPED and must be verified separately before a deliverable is declared fully conformant to STD-DP-19-005 v1.1.
How to read this table: Validator Disputes = how many GPKGs got FAIL from this script's automated checks. Cross-Checks & Diagnosis = probable root cause for each FAIL/PASS* result (by source software), plus a collapsible auditor cross-check panel with known blind spots and caveats. PASS* & Manual Verification = automated PASS* confirmation count, plus expandable manual inspection notes. Confidence: High = fully automated and confirmed; Medium = partial check or single known gap; Low = known validator bug or cannot be automated.

📌 Legend
TermDefinition
Validator Disputes Number of GeoPackages where this script returned FAIL for this requirement, out of total applicable GPKGs (non-SKIPPED)
Cross-Checks & Diagnosis Probable Cause — the likely root cause for any FAIL or PASS* result, broken down by source software (QGIS/GDAL vs ArcGIS) where relevant.
▶ Cross-check guidance (expandable) — current-run review guidance, misimplementations, or false-result risks for this requirement. Count shows how many applicable GPKGs were affected (FAIL or PASS* only).
PASS* & Manual Verification PASS* Manual Confirmed — how many of this requirement's PASS* results were subsequently confirmed by hands-on manual inspection.
▶ Manual Verification (expandable) — findings and notes from hands-on session analysis of the GeoPackage database.
High Confidence Check fully implements the ATS requirement, no known bugs, result confirmed manually
Medium Confidence Partial check (PASS*) or one known gap — result is likely correct but not fully verified
Low Confidence Known validator bug, logic error, or requirement cannot be automated — treat result with caution
PASS* Partial automated validation — structural or presence checks passed but content not fully verified. Two distinct causes: (1) the check is technically possible but this script only does part of it (e.g. WKT presence without content validation); (2) the check cannot be fully automated at all (e.g. Req 6, 13 content, 15 WKT structure, 17 epoch WKT, 18 XML values). See the ⚠️ section above for the full list of non-automatable requirements.
SKIPPED Requirement not applicable to this file (e.g. vector CRS check on a gridded-only file), OR the check was deliberately not automated because it requires external context (Req 6: product profile).
